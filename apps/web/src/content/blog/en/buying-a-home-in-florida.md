Buying in Florida is exciting, and it comes with its own challenges and opportunities. Here is what to know about the market, your mortgage options and the state's programs.

## Why buy in Florida

- **No state income tax**, which can leave more room in your budget.
- **A varied market:** beachfront condos in Miami, suburbs around Orlando, rural properties in North Florida.
- **A growing economy** in tourism, healthcare and technology.
- **Lifestyle** and many communities designed for active retirees.

## How the regions differ

- **South Florida (Miami, Fort Lauderdale):** high demand and higher prices.
- **Central Florida (Orlando, Tampa):** family suburbs, growing job markets and more affordable homes.
- **North Florida (Jacksonville, Tallahassee):** lower prices and a slower pace.
- **Coastal areas:** pricier, popular for vacation homes and rentals.

## Choose the right mortgage

- **Conventional:** higher credit and down payment requirements, competitive rates.
- **FHA:** for buyers with lower credit or smaller savings, with as little as 3.5% down from a 580 score.
- **VA:** for eligible veterans and service members, often with no down payment.
- **USDA:** for eligible rural and some suburban areas, with low or no down payment.

Not sure which fits? Try the [FHA vs. conventional vs. VA comparison](/en/tools/fha-vs-conventional-vs-va/). A broker compares lenders and programs for you, including options for lower credit.

## Florida-specific costs

- **Wind and flood insurance:** standard homeowners insurance does not cover floods. Flood insurance is often required in coastal and low-lying areas; check the FEMA flood map. These premiums can change what you can afford, so include them in the [payment calculator](/en/tools/payment-calculator/).
- **Property taxes and the Homestead Exemption:** if the home is your primary residence you can apply for the Homestead Exemption, which reduces the taxable value by up to $50,000.
- **Closing taxes:** Florida charges documentary stamp taxes and an intangible tax at closing. See them in the [closing cost calculator](/en/tools/florida-closing-costs/).

## Down payment help

The Florida Housing Finance Corporation runs programs for first-time buyers, including Hometown Heroes for essential workers such as teachers, first responders and healthcare workers. Funding and limits change during the year, so confirm what is open at floridahousing.org or ask us. Check your fit with the [Florida assistance checker](/en/tools/florida-assistance-checker/).

## Tips for first-time buyers

- Look into down payment assistance before you decide you can’t afford it.
- Budget for title insurance, appraisal and lender fees.
- Think about resale: growing areas tend to hold value.
- Work with a mortgage broker who can connect you with multiple lenders.
