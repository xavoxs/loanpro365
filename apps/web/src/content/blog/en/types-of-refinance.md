Not every refinance is the same. The right option depends on what you want to achieve and the loan you have today.

## Rate-and-term

You change the rate, the term or both without taking cash out. It’s the most common choice when rates drop or when you want to move from an adjustable to a fixed rate.

## Cash-out

The new loan is larger than what you owe and you receive the difference in cash. It’s used to consolidate high-interest debt or fund a renovation. As a reference, conventional and FHA cash-out loans can usually go up to 80% of the home’s value; VA loans may allow more for eligible veterans. Keep in mind you’re turning short-term debt into long-term debt secured by your home.

## FHA Streamline and VA IRRRL

If you already have an FHA or VA loan, simplified refinances let you lower your rate with less paperwork and, in many cases, no new appraisal:

- **FHA Streamline**, for FHA loans.
- **VA IRRRL** (Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan), for VA loans.

Both require the refinance to leave you with a net benefit, such as a lower payment.

## Conventional to remove mortgage insurance

If your home has appreciated and you have an FHA loan with lifetime mortgage insurance, moving to a conventional loan with at least 20% equity can remove that insurance.
