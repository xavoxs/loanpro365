Before looking for financing for an investment property or a business, you need to know which category it falls into. That determines the loan type, the requirements and the terms.

## The 1-to-4-unit rule

Properties with **1 to 4 units** (houses, condos, duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes) are financed as **residential loans**, even when you rent them out. These are the loans Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac and FHA buy or guarantee.

From **5 units** up, and buildings such as offices, retail, industrial, hotels or self-storage, are financed as **commercial loans**.

## Investors in 1-to-4 units: DSCR loans

If you’re buying to rent and don’t want to qualify on your personal income, a **DSCR** loan checks whether the property’s rent covers its payment. It’s residential by property type but underwritten as an investment. Test your deal with the [DSCR calculator](/en/tools/dscr-calculator/).

## Commercial properties

We finance income-producing real estate:

- Apartment buildings (5 units or more)
- Offices
- Retail and shopping centers
- Industrial buildings
- Hotels and motels
- Self-storage and other specialized properties
