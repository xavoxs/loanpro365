Beyond the down payment, you pay a set of charges on closing day. Knowing them ahead of time avoids buyers’ most common surprise.

## Florida taxes

- **Doc stamps on the note:** $0.35 per $100 of the loan (Fla. Stat. §201.08). Usually paid by the buyer.
- **Intangible tax:** 0.2% of the mortgage amount (Fla. Stat. §199.133). Also the buyer’s.
- **Doc stamps on the deed:** $0.70 per $100 of the price; in Miami-Dade, $0.60 for single-family homes and $1.05 for other property (Fla. Stat. §201.02 and §201.031). Usually paid by the seller, but negotiable in the contract.

Example: on a $320,000 loan, note doc stamps are $1,120 and the intangible tax is $640.

## Lender fees

Origination, processing, underwriting and, if you choose to buy down the rate, discount points. They all appear on your **Loan Estimate**, which you receive within three business days of applying.

## Title and settlement

Title search, title insurance, settlement company and recording fees. In Florida, who pays the owner’s title policy depends on the county and the contract.

## Prepaids and escrow

Interest from closing to your first payment, the first year of homeowners insurance and months of taxes and insurance to open your escrow account.
