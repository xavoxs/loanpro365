# Home (EN/ES) — surface brief

Scope: home page `/en/` and `/es/`; the same system restyles tools, content and pre-qualification pages. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: South Florida households (many Spanish-first, many on phones) unsure what they can afford. Action: start the 4-step pre-qualification; WhatsApp and phone secondary. Proof: NMLS numbers, real photo of Pablo (pending file), free tools, privacy promise (no SSN, no credit pull on site). No testimonials or stats exist: none invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard (Better.com / Rocket Mortgage craft) played straight: one number, one action. Refuses decorative stock-family heroes and hidden CTAs.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, deep navy ink #0c1f3f, one confident blue #1f4fd8 for every primary action, pale blue-gray fields #eef3fb, green only for confirmations. Manrope self-hosted, heavy display with tight tracking, tabular numerals. Rounded 12px controls, soft offset shadows, real line icons (Lucide).
STORY: In five seconds the visitor sees what this is (bilingual South Florida mortgage broker), gets a personal price range from two inputs, and is invited to pre-qualify; trust (NMLS, Pablo, privacy) sits next to the action, then how it works, loan paths, tools, Florida costs, Pablo, FAQ, final CTA.

Right 5/12: Pablo card with photo, name, NMLS, "Hablo español". Under both: one-line trust strip. Mobile: H1, estimator, CTA; Pablo card follows.
